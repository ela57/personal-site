# Graphic front

An extension of the Engineering Notebook, not the Trading Pit. Mode: Experience.

The hero is a 3:2 desk illustration with Ethan's reactive name on the left and a larger live wireframe below it on empty paper. Keep the static frame complete without motion. The introduction and project index follow the image rather than competing with it.

Renderer: wide illustration on the left with a smaller live evidence plate overlapping its right edge. Shader: illustration on the right, project text to its left; the case study uses the labelled illustration, not a flat browser simulation. Volatility: one quieter research spread with its real chart and no headline metric. Lab: a chess still-life lead image followed by Chess and strategy experiments.

Use the existing paper/ink/blue tokens and all three existing fonts. Illustration captions identify generated artwork; real evidence retains its own descriptive caption. All illustration assets are 1536 × 1024, with intrinsic dimensions and deliberate object positions. The supplied artwork is committed as quality-82 WebP with 768 × 512 responsive variants. The live hero wireframe sits below the name, separate from the drawing. Its caption stays inside the first desktop screen and flows below the artwork on phones.

Reactive name springs are the authored motion moment. Content is visible by default; no scroll reveals, decorative gradients or glows. Hover styling is limited to fine pointers. Keep existing Lab and overlay accessibility and bfcache guards.
