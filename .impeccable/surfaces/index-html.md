## Direction contract
THESIS: The portfolio is a trading floor seen from the visitors' gallery. Real SPY prices run on the board, Ethan's badge is the name, and each project is an order ticket on the floor. It refuses the dev-portfolio hero (headline, subcopy, two buttons, card grid) and the dark-terminal neon costume.
OWN-WORLD: Pit-floor charcoal #14161A ground; amber LED board #F2A30F rendered as a dot-matrix on canvas; jacket fields that own whole sections (exchange red #B3261E, floor green #2F6B4F, clerk blue #23408E); order-ticket card stock #E4DCC6 with ruled form lines, buy tickets in blue ink and sell tickets in red; laminated badges with three-letter acronyms. Archivo is the only face: Condensed 900 for badges, Expanded for the board header, Normal for prose, tabular numerals everywhere numbers live.
STORY: The visitor sees Ethan's badge and a board playing real SPY history, scrubs it, and watches MA-cross flags tick. They then read three tickets (renderer, shader, volatility study), each flipping open to its case study. Next they step into the Lab pits (chess, sandbox, chips), and the visit closes at the clearing desk with email and LinkedIn.
FIRST VIEWPORT: The top 62% is the price board, full width, in amber dot-matrix: SPY last/chg, a 20/100 SMA trace, and ticker-crawl rows. It is drag-scrubbable, and arrow keys step the day. Bottom-left, overlapping the board edge, is the badge ELM / ETHAN LAM at collision scale with a GT CmpE '28 sub-line. Bottom-right are three ticket stubs (01 Renderer / 02 Shader / 03 Vol), which are the primary action.
FORM: The Trading Pit, #4 on the ordered grounded list; seed:67e9534484cb19d7. Raises: collision-scale name; whole-cell grid snapping; ticket front = summary, flip = detail; every entry carries real dates.
