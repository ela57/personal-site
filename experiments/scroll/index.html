F15 · Sticky chess board — inspired by original idea

Philidor’s legacy

White · Nf7#

  1. White · Nh6+
  2. Black · Kh8
  3. White · Qg8+
  4. Black · Rxg8
  5. White · Nf7#

Moving the knight uncovers the queen’s diagonal and gives double check, forcing Kh8. The protected queen sacrifice then forces Rxg8, and Nf7 delivers smothered mate.
